Policy Switchboard

Recent work · Hybrid policy classifier

An applied ML lab that combines customer-specific MiniLM LoRA adapters with versioned business rules. A $15 refund can pass under a $20 limit and require review when the policy drops to $10.

  • Tenant-owned ticket context and redacted evidence through FastAPI
  • Evaluation gates before candidate activation, plus rollback support

Recorded frozen synthetic test: LoRA 66/66, zero unsafe approvals; matched frozen-encoder baseline 62/66, one unsafe approval. Facts and arithmetic are computed outside the neural model. Small synthetic evaluation; neural delivery remains in shadow research mode.
